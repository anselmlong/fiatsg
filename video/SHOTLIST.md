# Fiat launch film: shot list

60 fps, 52.0 s = 3120 frames. The 16:9 and 9:16 cuts share every beat; `timeline.mjs` holds the exact times.

## 0.0–3.0 s · Cold open: the flip
- **f0:** the face-down holy card is already falling toward camera out of night purple, with rays turning and embers drifting.
- **0.6 s:** it trembles harder and harder, the rays flare, and sparks gather in.
- **1.4 s (f84):** a white-gold flash, and the card flips in 3D on a spring: St Monica. A band of foil light sweeps across her.
- **2.2 s:** "You prayed. So did she." slams in.

## 3.0–7.2 s · The hook
Four 360° spins, each faster than the last. Each lands on a new saint with its line:
- Andrew: "You invited a friend."
- Lorenzo Ruiz: "You served migrants."
- Damien: "You cared for the sick."
- Anselm: "You asked a question."

At 5.6 s the camera pulls back hard. The card becomes one of 28 on cream: "28 saints to meet."

## 7.2–11.6 s · The problem
A hard cut to grey, with a low pulse. Three quick lines:
- "The Church needs helpers."
- "But the asks get buried." Group-chat scraps pile up, then blur away.
- "Can't make Sunday? Nothing for you."

## 11.6–15.2 s · The answer
One candle lights and warmth floods the frame. "Fiat — let it be done." draws in gold ink, then "An hour, a skill, a prayer or an invite."

## 15.2–40.2 s · The flow, on real screens
Each step has a numbered label beside (16:9) or above (9:16) a browser or phone showing the live site. The screen slowly pushes in on what matters, with a gold ring or a tap.

Saint cards drift like lanterns. "Not points. Companions." Then St Pier Giorgio Frassati's card comes forward over "Verso l'alto — to the heights!"

## 47.0–52.0 s · Call to action
The cards rush into a point of light, which blooms into the Fiat wordmark. Then:
- "What will your yes be?"
- "fiat.anselmlong.com"
- "Built for the FIAT Catholic Hackathon 2026"

The frame holds clean from 48.6 s. It's also the thumbnail.
